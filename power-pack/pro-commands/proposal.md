Draft a professional proposal:

1. **Executive summary** — the ask, the value, and the timeline in one paragraph
2. **Problem statement** — the specific pain point, quantified with data or stakes
3. **Proposed solution** — what you'll deliver, how it works, why this approach
4. **Scope** — explicit inclusions AND exclusions (prevents scope creep)
5. **Deliverables** — numbered list with acceptance criteria for each
6. **Timeline** — phased milestones with dates and dependencies
7. **Investment** — pricing with clear breakdown; payment terms; what's included vs. extra
8. **ROI case** — projected return, payback period, or value created
9. **Risk mitigation** — what could go wrong and your plan for each scenario
10. **Team & credentials** — relevant experience, case studies, or social proof
11. **Next steps** — specific action with a deadline to create urgency

Tone: confident, specific, focused on their outcomes not your process.
