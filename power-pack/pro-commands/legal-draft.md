Draft the following legal document. Structure it with:

1. **Definitions** — define all key terms precisely
2. **Core terms** — the substantive provisions appropriate to this document type
3. **Rights & obligations** — what each party can and must do
4. **Limitations of liability** — cap exposure appropriately for this context
5. **Intellectual property** — ownership, licenses granted, restrictions
6. **Data & privacy** — handling obligations, GDPR/CCPA considerations if applicable
7. **Term & termination** — duration, renewal, how to exit, what survives
8. **Dispute resolution** — governing law, arbitration vs. litigation, venue
9. **General provisions** — severability, entire agreement, amendment process, notices

Use clear, plain language where possible. Flag any clause that should be reviewed by a licensed attorney before use. Note: this is a starting draft, not legal advice.
