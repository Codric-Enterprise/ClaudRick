Build a crisis response plan:

1. **Situation assessment** — what happened, who knows, what's still unfolding
2. **Stakeholder map** — every audience affected, ranked by urgency (customers, employees, media, regulators, partners)
3. **Key messages** — 3 core messages that are true, empathetic, and action-oriented
4. **Holding statement** — ready-to-publish statement for immediate release (under 100 words)
5. **Full statement** — detailed response with facts, accountability, and next steps
6. **FAQ** — 10 hardest questions you'll face, with approved answers
7. **Channel plan** — where and when to communicate (social, email, press, internal)
8. **Spokesperson brief** — talking points, phrases to use, phrases to avoid, body language notes
9. **Timeline** — what to say at 1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours, 1 week
10. **Recovery plan** — how to rebuild trust after the immediate crisis passes

Tone: honest, empathetic, action-oriented. Never defensive or dismissive.
