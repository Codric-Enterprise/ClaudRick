Develop a brand identity:

1. **Brand essence** — one sentence that captures what makes this brand distinct
2. **Mission & vision** — why this exists and where it's going
3. **Target audience** — specific personas with demographics, psychographics, and pain points
4. **Brand personality** — 5 adjectives that describe how the brand feels, with examples of each in practice
5. **Voice & tone** — how the brand speaks: word choices, sentence style, formality level (with do/don't examples)
6. **Messaging hierarchy** — primary message, supporting messages, proof points
7. **Tagline options** — 5 candidates with rationale for each
8. **Visual direction** — color psychology, typography mood, imagery style (described, not designed)
9. **Competitive differentiation** — what no one else says or owns
10. **Brand guidelines summary** — one-page reference for anyone creating content

Ground everything in what the audience actually cares about, not what the brand wants to say about itself.
