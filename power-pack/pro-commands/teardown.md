description: Reverse-engineer how a product or system works
argument-hint: [product, feature, or system to analyze]
---
Reverse-engineer the following:

1. **What it does** — core functionality from a user's perspective
2. **How it likely works** — inferred architecture, technology stack, and key algorithms
3. **Data model** — what data it stores, how it's structured, where it likely lives
4. **Key technical decisions** — inferred trade-offs and why they were probably made
5. **Integration points** — external services, APIs, and third-party dependencies
6. **Business model mechanics** — how the technical architecture supports revenue
7. **Strengths** — what's well-built and hard to replicate
8. **Weaknesses** — architectural debt, scaling limits, single points of failure
9. **Build-vs-buy** — if you were recreating this, what would you build custom vs. use off-the-shelf
10. **Estimated effort** — team size, timeline, and cost to build a comparable system from scratch

Base analysis on observable behavior, public information, and industry patterns. Flag where you're speculating.
