Prepare a complete interview strategy:

1. **Role analysis** — key skills they're hiring for (read between the lines of the JD)
2. **Likely questions** — 10 most probable questions for this specific role, categorized (behavioral, technical, situational)
3. **STAR answers** — for each behavioral question, a structured Situation-Task-Action-Result framework with specific, quantified results
4. **Technical deep-dives** — topics they'll probe and how to demonstrate depth without rambling
5. **Questions to ask them** — 5 questions that signal competence and genuine interest (not generic)
6. **Company research brief** — recent news, product direction, competitive position, culture signals
7. **Red flags to watch for** — signals this role might not be what it seems
8. **Salary negotiation** — market range for this role, how to deflect early salary questions, anchoring strategy
9. **Opening statement** — your 60-second "tell me about yourself" tailored to this role
10. **Closing statement** — how to end strong and set up next steps
