description: Facilitate a team retrospective with structured exercises
argument-hint: [sprint, project, or period to reflect on]
---
Facilitate a retrospective:

1. **Context setting** — summarize the period: goals, key events, outcomes
2. **Data gathering** — structured prompts for the team:
   - What went well? (celebrate and reinforce)
   - What didn't go well? (identify without blame)
   - What surprised us? (surface hidden assumptions)
   - What did we learn? (extract transferable insights)
3. **Pattern recognition** — group the feedback into themes; highlight recurring issues
4. **Root cause analysis** — for the top 3 issues, dig into systemic causes (not surface symptoms)
5. **Action items** — specific, assigned, time-boxed improvements (max 3 to ensure follow-through)
6. **Metrics check** — velocity, quality, predictability trends across recent periods
7. **Team health** — energy, collaboration, psychological safety signals
8. **Follow-up on previous actions** — did last retro's action items actually happen?

Keep it blameless and forward-looking.
