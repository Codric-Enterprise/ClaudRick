Conduct a UX review:

1. **First impressions** — what a new user understands (and misunderstands) in the first 5 seconds
2. **Task analysis** — for the primary user goal, every click/step required vs. the minimum possible
3. **Heuristic evaluation** — score against Nielsen's 10 heuristics with specific violations
4. **Information architecture** — is the navigation logical? Can users find what they need?
5. **Visual hierarchy** — does the eye flow to the right elements in the right order?
6. **Accessibility** — WCAG 2.1 AA violations: contrast, focus states, alt text, keyboard navigation
7. **Copy audit** — labels, error messages, and CTAs that confuse or mislead
8. **Mobile experience** — touch targets, responsive behavior, thumb-zone placement
9. **Cognitive load** — where the user has to think too hard, and how to reduce it
10. **Priority fixes** — top 5 changes ranked by impact/effort, with mockup descriptions

Be specific. "Improve the button" is not useful — "Change the CTA from 'Submit' to 'Get my report' and increase contrast ratio from 2.8:1 to 4.5:1" is.
