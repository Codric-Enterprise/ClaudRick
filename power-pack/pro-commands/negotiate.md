Prepare a complete negotiation strategy:

1. **BATNA** — your best alternative if this negotiation fails; how to strengthen it
2. **Their BATNA** — the other side's likely alternative; their pressure points
3. **ZOPA** — the zone of possible agreement; your walk-away point vs. their likely limits
4. **Anchor** — your opening position and the reasoning that justifies it
5. **Concession plan** — what you can give up (ordered by cost to you vs. value to them)
6. **Key arguments** — your three strongest points, with evidence and framing
7. **Anticipated objections** — what they'll push back on and your prepared responses
8. **Tactics** — specific techniques for this situation (silence, bracketing, deadline pressure, etc.)
9. **Script** — opening statement, key phrases, and closing language ready to use
10. **Red lines** — non-negotiables and how to communicate them firmly without killing the deal
