Review the following contract or agreement. Produce:

1. **Key obligations** — what each party must do, with deadlines
2. **Risk flags** — clauses that are unusually aggressive, one-sided, or ambiguous (quote the exact language)
3. **Missing protections** — standard clauses that are absent (limitation of liability, indemnification, termination rights, IP ownership, confidentiality, dispute resolution)
4. **Financial terms** — payment schedule, penalties, auto-renewal traps, price escalation
5. **Termination analysis** — how to exit, notice periods, what survives termination
6. **IP & data** — who owns what, license grants, data handling obligations
7. **Negotiation leverage** — which clauses to push back on and specific alternative language to propose
8. **Plain-English summary** — what this contract actually means in 3-4 sentences

Flag anything that requires legal counsel before signing.
