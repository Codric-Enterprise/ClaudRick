Design a monetization strategy for the following:

1. **Revenue model options** — rank by fit: subscription, freemium, one-time, usage-based, marketplace, advertising, licensing
2. **Pricing analysis** — competitor pricing, willingness-to-pay estimate, price anchoring strategy
3. **Tier structure** — free vs. paid tiers with specific feature gates that drive upgrades
4. **Unit economics** — CAC, LTV, payback period, gross margin targets
5. **Conversion funnel** — free → trial → paid journey with specific triggers at each stage
6. **Pricing page** — copy, layout, and psychological framing (decoy pricing, annual discount)
7. **Revenue projections** — month 1-12 with assumptions clearly stated
8. **Payment infrastructure** — which platform (Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy) and why
9. **Expansion revenue** — upsell, cross-sell, and usage-based growth opportunities
10. **Risks** — what kills revenue and how to hedge each risk

Be specific to this product's market. No generic advice.
