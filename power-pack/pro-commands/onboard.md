Design a complete onboarding experience:

1. **First impression** — what happens in the first 30 seconds (the "aha" moment)
2. **Day 1 journey** — step-by-step flow with specific screens, emails, or actions
3. **Progressive disclosure** — what to show immediately vs. unlock over time
4. **Activation metric** — the single action that predicts long-term retention
5. **Friction audit** — every point where users might drop off and how to reduce each
6. **Welcome sequence** — email/notification cadence for days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30
7. **Empty states** — how to make blank screens useful instead of intimidating
8. **Social proof** — where and how to show that others succeeded
9. **Checklist** — user-facing progress tracker with quick wins front-loaded
10. **Measurement** — funnel metrics, cohort analysis setup, and improvement targets

Be specific to this product and audience. Include copy for key moments.
