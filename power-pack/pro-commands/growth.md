Build a growth strategy:

1. **Growth model** — map the full funnel: awareness → acquisition → activation → retention → revenue → referral
2. **North star metric** — the single metric that captures core value delivery
3. **Channel analysis** — rank acquisition channels by estimated CAC, volume, and time to results
4. **Top 3 channels** — deep dive into your best-fit channels with specific tactics and budgets
5. **Viral mechanics** — built-in sharing loops, referral incentives, network effects
6. **Retention levers** — what brings users back: notifications, content, habit loops, switching costs
7. **Experiment backlog** — 10 growth experiments ranked by ICE score (impact, confidence, ease)
8. **Content strategy** — topics, formats, and distribution that attract your ideal user
9. **Partnerships** — specific companies or communities to partner with and the pitch for each
10. **90-day plan** — week-by-week priorities with measurable targets

Every tactic must tie to a metric. No vanity metrics.
