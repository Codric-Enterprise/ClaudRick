Write landing page copy that converts:

1. **Headline** — one line that communicates the core benefit (not the feature)
2. **Subheadline** — expand on the headline; address the target audience directly
3. **Hero CTA** — button text and surrounding urgency/trust signals
4. **Problem section** — describe the pain in the reader's own words
5. **Solution section** — how your product solves it (benefits, not features)
6. **Social proof** — testimonial templates, trust badges, metrics to display
7. **Feature breakdown** — 3-6 features, each as a benefit statement with supporting detail
8. **Objection handling** — FAQ that addresses the top 5 reasons people don't buy
9. **Pricing section** — copy around the price that frames value (not cost)
10. **Final CTA** — closing section with urgency, risk reversal (guarantee), and clear next step

Write in the voice of the target customer's trusted advisor. Specific > clever.
