Produce a competitive analysis:

1. **Landscape map** — all significant competitors categorized (direct, indirect, emerging)
2. **Feature matrix** — side-by-side comparison on the dimensions that matter to buyers
3. **Positioning** — where each player sits on the axes that define this market
4. **Strengths & weaknesses** — for each competitor, what they do well and where they're vulnerable
5. **Pricing comparison** — tier structures, price points, and what each includes
6. **Differentiation opportunities** — gaps no one owns that you could claim
7. **Competitive moats** — what makes each player hard to displace (network effects, switching costs, data, brand)
8. **Win/loss patterns** — when customers choose competitor X over Y, what drives it
9. **Recommended positioning** — your specific angle, messaging, and proof points
10. **Battlecard** — one-page sales reference: key objections and responses for each competitor
