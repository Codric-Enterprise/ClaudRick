description: Check compliance against a regulation or standard
argument-hint: [system/product + regulation — e.g., "our SaaS app, GDPR"]
---
Audit the following for compliance:

1. **Applicable requirements** — specific articles/sections that apply to this system
2. **Current state assessment** — compliant, partially compliant, or non-compliant for each requirement
3. **Gap analysis** — what's missing, ranked by risk (regulatory fine, legal liability, reputational damage)
4. **Remediation plan** — specific technical and process changes for each gap
5. **Documentation needs** — policies, procedures, and records required
6. **Data mapping** — what personal/sensitive data flows where, retention periods, legal basis
7. **Third-party risk** — vendor compliance obligations and how to verify them
8. **Timeline** — realistic remediation schedule with dependencies
9. **Ongoing obligations** — recurring audits, reporting, training requirements

Reference specific regulation sections. Flag anything that needs legal counsel.
