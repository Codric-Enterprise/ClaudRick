Build a product roadmap:

1. **Vision statement** — where this product is heading in one sentence
2. **Strategic pillars** — 3-4 themes that group the work and tie to business goals
3. **Now / Next / Later** — features categorized by urgency with rationale for each placement
4. **Priority scoring** — RICE or ICE score for the top 15 candidates (show the math)
5. **Dependencies** — what blocks what; critical path highlighted
6. **Resource requirements** — team size, skills needed, external dependencies for each major item
7. **Milestones** — key dates with specific deliverables (not vague phases)
8. **Success metrics** — how to measure whether each initiative worked
9. **Risks & bets** — what you're betting on and the evidence supporting each bet
10. **Cut list** — what you're explicitly NOT doing and why (prevents scope creep)

Tie every feature to a user outcome or business metric. No feature islands.
