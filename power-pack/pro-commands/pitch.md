Build a compelling pitch structure for the following:

1. **Hook** — one sentence that makes the audience lean in
2. **Problem** — the pain point, with a specific story or data point that makes it real
3. **Solution** — what you built and why it's different (not features — the insight)
4. **Demo moment** — the single most impressive thing to show, and how to frame it
5. **Market** — TAM/SAM/SOM with sources, or a bottoms-up estimate if novel
6. **Traction** — what metrics prove this is working (or what milestones validate the thesis)
7. **Business model** — how money flows, unit economics, path to profitability
8. **Team** — why this team wins (specific unfair advantages)
9. **Ask** — what you need, what you'll do with it, and the milestone it unlocks
10. **Closing line** — memorable, ties back to the hook

For each slide: the key message (one sentence), supporting data, and speaker notes.
